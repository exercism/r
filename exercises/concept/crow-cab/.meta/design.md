# Design

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to teach the student the basics of using complex numbers in R.

## Learning objectives

- Understand how to instantiate a complex number.
- Understand how to subtract complex numbers.
- Understand how to use the `Re()` and `Im()` functions.
- Optionally, understand how to use `abs()` to find the magnitude of a complex number.
- Optionally, understand how to use `Conj()` function.

## Out of scope

- Complex multiplication
- Rotations with complex numbers

## Concepts

This exercise unlocks no other concepts.

## Prerequisites

- `functions`
